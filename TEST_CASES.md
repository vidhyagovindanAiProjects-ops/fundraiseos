## Meeting-note extraction

1. Open LP Brain.
2. Click **Upload meeting note**.
3. Use the sample Nora Ellis note.
4. Confirm review/edit JSON appears.
5. Click **Approve and update LP Brain**.
6. Confirm the LP profile, follow-up task, activity feed, relationship graph, dashboard metrics, and Ask Memory context update.
