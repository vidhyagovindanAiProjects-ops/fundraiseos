# LP Brain Demo Script

## What to say

“LP Brain is an AI fundraising chief of staff. It understands the fund, identifies the right LPs, captures every interaction, recommends the next best action, and helps emerging venture funds close capital faster.”

## What to click

1. Start on the dashboard and show the hero headline.
2. Open **Fund DNA**.
3. Click **Use sample fund materials**.
4. Approve the structured Fund DNA JSON.
5. Show **Best-Fit LPs**.
6. Open Elena Park and explain the LP Fit Intelligence.
7. Open Ask Memory.
8. Ask:
   - Which LPs are the best fit for this fund?
   - Which LPs should the GP prioritize this week?
   - Draft a first outreach email for Elena Park.
9. Open **Upload meeting note**.
10. Use the sample Nora Ellis note.
11. Approve the extracted JSON.
12. Show the updated LP profile, follow-up, graph, and Ask Memory context.

## Known limitations

- Real extraction requires `OPENAI_API_KEY`.
- TXT and pasted text are the most reliable demo inputs.
- The demo dataset is local and resets with **Reset demo**.
